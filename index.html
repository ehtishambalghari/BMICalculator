Health disclaimer

BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. It does not directly measure body fat or determine an individual's overall health. Standard adult BMI categories are not intended for children and teenagers, whose results require age- and sex-specific interpretation. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for personalized guidance.
